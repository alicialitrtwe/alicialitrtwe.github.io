Bayesian

An area, not a project

What I mean by this heading is narrower than it sounds. Not Bayesian as a school — Bayesian as the discipline of asking what a fitted parameter is actually entitled to mean.

Why it is a category

A model can predict well and still support no inference at all. If the features it is fit on are correlated enough, many different parameter settings produce identical predictions, and the fitted weights are not a measurement of anything — they are one arbitrary point in a set the data cannot distinguish between. Prediction accuracy will not warn you about this. Nothing in the fit will.

So the work in this area is about the conditions under which a fitted model is allowed to be read: identifiability, sparsity, and priors chosen so that the basis a thing is expressed in is one where the coordinates mean something separately. The neuroscience it gets applied to sits under cognition; what makes it a category of its own is the question about the estimate rather than the question about the brain.
